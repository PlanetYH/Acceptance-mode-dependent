## Interpretation cautions

- The radial-only model is an ordinary least-squares polynomial of degree four
  in normalized combined NCPA RMS. It is not a spline, interpolant, local
  regression, or analytic quadratic law.
- Variance closure is a finite-sample statistical check. The separately
  estimated `ddof=1` terms need not close as an exact algebraic identity.
- The radial-content analysis is observational within the generated Sauvage-type
  screen ensemble. It reports association, not a causal controlled-mode
  injection result.
- Discovery and validation denote independently seeded libraries generated by
  the same physical/statistical model, not external experimental validation.
